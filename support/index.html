Support

Need help with Elemix? Email support@ardasnturk.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I combine materials?

Drag one card onto another, or tap two cards one after the other.

When do the clock and the thermometer unlock?

The day/night clock on the left edge unlocks at level 4, the temperature slider on the right edge at level 8.

What does a hint do?

A hint shows the next step of the shortest solution. New players get 3 hints; more can be bought in the hint shop (light bulb button).

I bought hints but they did not arrive.

Close and reopen the app while online: Elemix checks your purchases at launch and credits any hints that are missing. If they still do not appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change the language?

Tap the globe button on the level map. It opens the iOS Settings page for Elemix, where you can choose the app language.
