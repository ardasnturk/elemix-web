Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information the Elemix iPhone app (“Elemix”, “the app”) uses. Elemix is made by Arda Senturk. In short: Elemix has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your game progress stays on your device.

Information we do not collect

Elemix does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Game progress

Your progress (levels, stars, discovered materials and your hint balance) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Hint packs are sold as in-app purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. To validate purchases and credit hints, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device, your purchase history for Elemix (products, prices, dates, transaction IDs), and basic technical information such as the app version, device model, operating system and country/storefront. This information is used only to make purchases work and is not used for advertising. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Elemix is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete all game data at any time by deleting the app. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, or any other privacy question, contact us at support@ardasnturk.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
